Why this conversation

We learn to earn, but what about managing money?

We talk about earning money. But do we talk enough about managing it?

Financial independence isn't just about how much you earn. It's about understanding where your money goes, preparing for uncertainty and making informed decisions about your future.

For women in law, an independent practice, irregular income and delayed payments can make financial planning challenging. That's why understanding your finances matters.

Because earning is one step. Knowing what to do with it is another.

Points for discussion

What we'll talk about

  • Budgeting and savings

    Understand your income, expenses and saving habits.

  • Financial security

    Build an emergency fund and understand the basics of insurance.

  • Investing fundamentals

    Learn about risk, time horizons and informed financial decisions.

  • Planning ahead

    Take practical steps towards your financial goals.
